KORENIEVA TAMARA

Passing By…

Sometimes you are simply going about your day, not looking for anything special. And suddenly, your attention is drawn to something — or someone. A gesture, a glance, an incidental detail… And all at once, you see something in it that goes beyond an ordinary passing moment. It happens in a curious way, entirely beyond your control. Sometimes you notice such moments; other times, you walk right past without seeing anything at all. As if someone unseen occasionally turns on a few frames of a quiet, deeply personal film just for you…

These photographs capture precisely those moments — the ones I simply couldn’t walk past.
