KORENIEVA TAMARA

Sviatohirsk, Ukraine — Before 2022

Sviatohirsk is a small town in the Donetsk region, a place I always returned to with a special feeling. The chalk hills, the gentle flow of the Siverskyi Donets River, the forests, lakes, and monastery… All of this created a unique atmosphere of peace, harmony, and beauty. It was a place where I felt especially close to nature, where I wanted to pause, take in the surroundings, photograph, and simply be.

Now these photographs preserve for me not only the beauty of Sviatohirsk, but also the memory of a world that was dear to me. The war has brought destruction here, and it is very difficult to accept what has happened to a place that meant so much to me. I leave these photographs here as a tribute to Sviatohirsk, as I knew and loved it. The Sviatohirsk that will forever remain in my heart.
