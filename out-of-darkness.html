KORENIEVA TAMARA

Out of Darkness.

Black is not a color. In physics, it is the absence of light, the absence of color. Emptiness.
Perhaps that is why black feels so close to those states in which we seem to feel nothing at all:
sadness, exhaustion, silence… And yet, even in black, light always appears.
Faint. Quiet. It touches petals, droplets, glass — and suddenly, color is born.
