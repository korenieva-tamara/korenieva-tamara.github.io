KORENIEVA TAMARA

My heart is with them forever…

During the two years I spent volunteering at the hospital, I met many young men. Each of them had their own story, their own pain, their own struggles, and their own path forward.

I remember every one of them. Our conversations, the moments we shared, our walks, photographs, songs… I remember their smiles and their eyes, the moments when things felt a little easier, and those when I especially wanted to be there for them.

I love each and every one of them. And I am genuinely happy whenever someone finds their place in this new chapter of life as a veteran, discovers new opportunities, returns to something they love, or finds something entirely new. I rejoice in every step forward, big or small.

And it hurts deeply when things don’t work out, when life brings someone yet another difficult challenge. Because behind every one of these stories, I see not just someone I once knew, but a person who has become close to my heart.

Perhaps this is the most precious thing I have taken away from those two years: people do not disappear from our lives simply because our days together have come to an end or circumstances have changed.

They remain in our memories, in photographs, in videos, in warm recollections. And in our hearts.

Thank you, each and every one of you, for our meeting. For your trust. For allowing me to be a small part of your lives.
