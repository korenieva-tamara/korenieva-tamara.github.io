KORENIEVA TAMARA

About Me

A World of Beauty, Seen Through My Eyes

I came to photography after retirement, when a simple curiosity gradually grew into a passion that opened up a whole new world to me.

It all began with a small Pentax Optio compact camera. Soon, encouraged by the person who sold it to me, I took a step further and bought my first entry-level camera.

I discovered that photography was not merely about capturing what I saw — it was about learning to notice what might otherwise go unseen.

Just three years later, in 2012, I won second and third prizes in the amateur Macro category of the All-Ukrainian Photographer of the Year 2012 photography competition. Macro photography became my special love.

The delicate structure of a flower, a tiny insect resting on a leaf, the sparkle of morning dew — details that are easy to overlook became an endless source of wonder and inspiration.

In 2014, the war forced me to leave my home in Donetsk. Since then, my life has been marked by uncertainty, never knowing what tomorrow might bring. Perhaps this is one of the reasons I wanted to create this website: to bring together a part of my creative journey and preserve something meaningful in a life where so much has changed.

The photographs presented here are only a small selection of my work. They reflect different moments in my journey, my love of nature, and my continuing search for beauty and artistic expression.

Alongside macro photography and still lifes, I have developed my own artistic approach, which I call Photo-Watercolors — a meeting point between photography and painting.

For me, photography is more than documenting what I see. It is a way of expressing how I feel, of discovering beauty in the smallest things, and of sharing a little of that wonder with others.

Thank you for being here and seeing the world through my eyes.
