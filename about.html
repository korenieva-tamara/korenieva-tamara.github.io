KORENIEVA TAMARA

About Me

🇺🇦 I am a Ukrainian photographer, living and creating in Ukraine.
It is now 2026, and I am 72 years old.

In 2014, I was forced to leave my home in Donetsk with just one suitcase and my cat. Along with my home, I left behind everything from my past life. Since then, my life has been marked by war, displacement, and uncertainty about what tomorrow may bring.

I came to photography after retirement. What began as simple curiosity gradually grew into a passion that opened up an entirely new world for me.

It all started with a small Pentax Optio compact camera. Soon, encouraged by the young man who sold it to me, I took the next step and bought my first entry-level camera. I discovered that photography was not simply a way to capture what I saw, but an opportunity to learn to notice what usually goes unnoticed.

Just three years later, in 2012, I won second and third prizes in the amateur Macro category of the All-Ukrainian Photographer of the Year 2012 competition.

Macro photography became a special love of mine. The delicate structure of a flower, a tiny insect resting on a leaf, morning dew sparkling in the sunlight — details that are so easy to overlook became an inexhaustible source of wonder and inspiration for me.

Alongside macro photography and still lifes, I developed my own artistic approach, which I call Photo-Watercolors — a blend of photography and painting.

The photographs presented here are only a small selection of my work. They reflect different moments along my journey, my love of nature, and my ongoing search for beauty and artistic expression.

For me, photography is more than capturing what I see. It is a way to express my feelings, discover beauty in the smallest things, and share that sense of wonder with others.

Thank you for visiting and seeing the world through my eyes.
